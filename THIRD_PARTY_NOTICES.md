## Bundled data and separate programs

### ffmpeg (separate program, LGPL v2.1 or later)

The app starts `ffmpeg` (`ffmpeg.exe` on Windows) as a separate process to add
overlays to videos, convert HEVC videos to H.264 and join clips. Releases ship
NovaByteWorks' own build of FFmpeg 9.0.2 configured with only the components the
app uses, without `--enable-gpl` and without `--enable-nonfree`. FFmpeg is licensed
under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later; the full text is
below and is installed next to the program together with `SOURCE_OFFER.txt`, which
says where the complete corresponding source, the build script and the configure
options are published (the `ffmpeg/` folder of each release at
<https://github.com/arbaaz360/memories-rescue-releases>) and makes the written
offer required by the licence. Nothing from FFmpeg is linked into the app and the
user may replace the program with another build. Development builds of the app
may instead carry the BtbN FFmpeg build (LGPL v3, see `third_party/README.md`);
those builds are not released.

FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.

### Time zone boundary data (ODbL-1.0)

The time zone boundary data bundled in this application (via the `tzf-rs` crate
and its `tzf-rel` data package) is made available under the Open Database License
(ODbL) v1.0, <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/>, whose text is below.
It is derived from timezone-boundary-builder by Evan Siroky
(<https://github.com/evansiroky/timezone-boundary-builder>), which is in turn derived
from OpenStreetMap data, copyright OpenStreetMap contributors

the precision reduction performed by the tzf project; no derivative database is

The IANA Time Zone Database used for daylight-saving rules is in the public domain.
